# Privacy policy

**PoE Market Monitor**

**Effective date: 4 October 2026**

**Developer:** Vladislav Ignatjev

**Privacy contact:** [vladislavignatjev88@gmail.com](mailto:vladislavignatjev88@gmail.com)

PoE Market Monitor is a mobile application for viewing Path of Exile 1 and
Path of Exile 2 market information. It is operated by its individual developer,
Vladislav Ignatjev. This policy explains what the app stores on your device and
what may be shared with third parties when you use it.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores the following data locally so its features work:

- favorite market items and alert rules;
- selected game, league, theme, and alert-pause settings;
- your selected age band (under 13, 13–17, or 18+), used on this device to
  apply the app's age restriction and suitable ad and consent settings; no date
  of birth is requested;
- market quotes and price observations used for cached results and price
  history; and
- local notification history.

This information is stored in Android app preferences and a local SQLite
database. The app has no account or sign-in feature and does not send this
information to the app developer. Price observations and notification history
are pruned on the device (up to 30 days, with additional record limits).

## Market data and network information

The app requests public market data from poe.ninja over HTTPS. Requests include
the selected game, league, and market category. poe.ninja and network providers
may receive connection information such as your IP address and may process it
under their own privacy policies. The app does not include your favorites,
alerts, or theme settings in these market requests. Market responses are
cached locally on your device.

## Notifications

The app can request Android notification permission when you create an alert.
Alerts are evaluated on your device, and notifications and their history are
local. The app does not use push notification tokens or send alert contents to
the developer.

## Advertising

The Play release displays ads using Google Mobile Ads. Google and its
advertising partners may
process approximate location inferred from your IP address; app interactions
such as launches, taps, and ad views; diagnostics about app or SDK performance;
and device or account identifiers such as Android Advertising ID and App Set
ID (other account identifiers may apply on signed-in devices). Google describes
these SDK data flows as supporting advertising, analytics, and fraud
prevention. Data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit
using TLS. Google's disclosures and policies describe this processing in more
detail, and govern Google's handling of this data. Before market or ad network
requests begin, the app asks you to select an age band. The choice is stored
locally and can be changed in Settings. The app does not request a date of
birth. Under-13 users cannot continue. For ages 13–17, UMP is told to apply
under-age-of-consent handling and Google Mobile Ads is configured for teen
treatment. Google receives age-treatment flags with those ad and consent
requests, but not your date of birth. All ad requests are limited to
general-audience content. For adults,
the app refreshes consent information using UMP, presents any configured form
that UMP says is required, and checks UMP's permission before initializing the
ad SDK or loading a banner. When UMP says a privacy-options entry point is
required, "Ads privacy options" in Settings lets you reopen the form and change
your choices. The developer must also configure the appropriate privacy
messages in AdMob for the regions where the app is distributed.

You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID in Android's privacy or
advertising settings. Google's privacy controls provide additional information
about managing data associated with Google services.

Google privacy information: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>

Google Play advertising ID information:

## Data sharing and security

The app developer does not receive or share the locally stored app data. Market
requests go to poe.ninja. When ads are enabled, ad requests go to Google Mobile
Ads and its partners. The app does not currently include developer-operated
accounts, cloud synchronization, analytics, crash reporting, or a backend
service.

Market requests use HTTPS. Data stored by the app remains within the app's
local storage and is subject to Android device security and backup settings.

## Retention and deletion

You can remove app data through Android Settings by clearing PoE Market
Monitor's storage, or remove it by uninstalling the app. This deletes the
app's local preferences and database from the device, subject to Android
backup behavior. Local price observations and notification history are also
pruned automatically. There is no account or developer-held app data to delete
through an online account-deletion service.

## Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not designed for
children under 13. Under-13 users cannot continue past the age screen. The app
stores only the selected age band locally, not a date of birth; the developer
does not receive the age selection. For users aged 13–17, ad requests use teen
treatment, under-age-of-consent handling, and a general-audience ad-content
limit.
